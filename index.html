Important protocol status

The exact Beacon Plus-BL firmware's advertisement payload has not been independently confirmed here. The app can inspect real advertisements and test a documented-looking Minew ACC candidate parser, but it will only publish sensor values after payload shape and plausibility checks. If the beacon does not expose a usable advertisement stream in your browser, this app will report that rather than guessing a GATT characteristic or decoding encrypted data.

Web Bluetooth requires a supported browser and HTTPS or localhost. Chrome/Edge desktop are the best first test. iPhone Safari generally does not support Web Bluetooth. Advertisement scanning support varies by browser. Speech synthesis may be unavailable or require a user gesture.
